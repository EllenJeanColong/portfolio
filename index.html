Featured Project · Independent Initiative

Invoice Monitoring & Follow-Up Automation

The challenge. Repetitive invoice checks, manual follow-up preparation, and fragmented status reporting made unresolved discrepancies more difficult to track consistently.

My approach. I designed an integrated workflow using Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Slack to validate records, prepare reviewable follow-up drafts, maintain case tracking, and support reporting and notifications.

  • Required-field checks and duplicate identification
  • Structured email draft preparation with human review
  • Invoice and email-thread follow-up tracking
  • Dashboard summaries and escalation visibility

Business value. Less repetitive preparation, more consistent checks, clearer ownership, and better visibility into pending work. These are qualitative outcomes; no unverified time-saving percentages are claimed.
